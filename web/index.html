Blacklight Bouldering

上海 · 黑光抱石 — an evening of climbing under UV light.

About the evening

For one evening, the gym lights at Banana Bouldering are switched off and the wall is lit with UV light only. The holds and route tape glow, the room stays dark, and climbers have 90 minutes on the wall.

Entry is free. Up to 100 people can climb at a time.

Programme

19:00Entry. Liquid chalk and shoe sizes at the desk.
19:15Climbing starts. 90 minutes on the wall.
21:00End of the event. The gym lights come back on.

Who can take part

The evening is open to every level, including first-timers.

First timeJugs and a traverse. We will point at them.
VB – V2You climb the easy wall at the gym.
V3 – V5Regular sessions. You will have plenty.
V6 and upA few UV problems are genuinely hard.

Included

  • 90 minutes on the UV wall
  • Liquid chalk
  • Shoe rental, EU 35–47

Rules on the wall

  • No torches, phone lights, or camera flash on the wall.
  • White cotton glows. Dark clothes disappear.
  • Bouldering only. Keep your feet over the mat.
  • One person on a problem at a time.
  • You need to be 16 or older.

Location

Banana Bouldering (香蕉抱石), Shanghai. Entry is at 19:00, and shoe sizes are sorted at the desk.
